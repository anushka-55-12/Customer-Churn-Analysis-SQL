*# Customer Churn Analysis*



*## Project Overview*



*Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges faced by telecom companies. Losing customers not only affects revenue but also increases the cost of acquiring new customers.*



*In this project, I analyzed customer churn data using SQL and business analysis techniques to identify the key factors influencing customer attrition and provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.*



*---*



*## Objective*



*The main objective of this project was to answer important business questions such as:*



*- Which customers are most likely to churn?*

*- Does contract type influence churn?*

*- Which payment methods are associated with higher churn?*

*- How does internet service impact customer retention?*

*- Which customer groups require more attention from the retention team?*



*---*



*## Dataset*



*Dataset Used:*

*IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset*



*The dataset contains customer information such as:*



*- Customer demographics*

*- Contract details*

*- Payment methods*

*- Internet services*

*- Monthly charges*

*- Churn status*



*Total Customers: 7043*

*## Business Questions Answered*



*### 1. What is the overall customer churn rate?*



*Analyzed the total number of customers and churned customers to understand the overall churn situation.*



*### 2. Which contract type has the highest churn?*



*Compared churn across different contract categories to identify high-risk customer segments.*



*### 3. Which payment method is associated with the highest churn?*



*Analyzed customer churn based on payment preferences.*



*### 4. Which internet service has the highest churn?*



*Compared churn behavior among DSL, Fiber Optic, and customers without internet service.*



*### 5. Which customer tenure group is most likely to churn?*



*Segmented customers based on tenure to understand customer lifecycle behavior.*



*### 6. Which contract type contributes the highest revenue loss?*



*Estimated revenue impact caused by customer churn.*



*---*



*## Key Findings*



*- Month-to-Month customers had the highest churn rate.*

*- Electronic Check users showed the highest churn behavior.*

*- Fiber Optic customers contributed the largest share of churn cases.*

*- New customers were more likely to leave than long-term customers.*

*- Churn resulted in significant monthly revenue loss.*



*---*



*## Business Recommendations*



*- Encourage customers to switch to long-term contracts.*

*- Promote automatic payment methods.*

*- Improve onboarding experiences for new customers.*

*- Investigate churn drivers among Fiber Optic customers.*

*- Launch targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customer groups.*
